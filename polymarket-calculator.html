How to find mispriced prediction-market contracts

On Polymarket, Kalshi, and other prediction markets, a contract price is a probability: 40¢ means the market thinks there's a 40% chance. That makes finding an edge simple in principle — estimate the true probability of the outcome, then buy contracts priced below it and fade contracts priced above it.

The cleanest way to estimate true probability is to de-vig the sharpest sportsbook lines. Sportsbooks bake in a margin, so the two sides' implied probabilities add up to more than 100%. Removing that margin proportionally gives you a fair, no-vig probability for each side — the number to hold up against a contract price.

Why prediction markets get mispriced

Prediction-market prices reflect crowd sentiment as much as probability. Brand-name and popular teams carry a premium because casual money piles onto recognizable names, while small-market teams and underdogs get underpriced. That gap between perception and true probability is the edge — and it's biggest during high-attention events like playoffs and finals, when emotional money floods in.
