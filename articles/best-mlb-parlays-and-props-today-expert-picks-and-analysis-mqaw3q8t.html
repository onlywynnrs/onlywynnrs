Building Profitable MLB Parlays with Sharp Discipline

The biggest mistake recreational bettors make with MLB parlays is chasing long odds without proper correlation. A five-leg parlay at +2000 might look enticing, but if those legs don't have any logical connection, you're just lighting money on fire. Smart parlay construction means understanding how outcomes influence each other.

Same-game parlays offer the best correlation opportunities. If you believe a starting pitcher will dominate, combine his strikeout prop with the under and his team's run line. If you're targeting a slugfest, pair the over with multiple hitter props and the underdog's alternate spread. This approach gives you multiple ways to win when your primary thesis hits.

For multi-game parlays, focus on game totals and run lines where you have the strongest convictions. Avoid mixing money lines at heavy chalk prices—the juice kills your expected value. Instead, look for underdogs with sharp action or run-line value on favorites where you're getting a better number.
