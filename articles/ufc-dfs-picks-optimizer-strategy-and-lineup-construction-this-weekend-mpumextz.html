Optimizer Settings and Lineup Construction Techniques

Most DFS players make the mistake of running their optimizer once and submitting those lineups. Winning players understand that optimizer settings require customization based on contest type, field size, and your confidence level in specific fighters.

For cash games, use tighter projection ranges and prioritize fighters with high floors. Set your optimizer to minimize variance by favoring decision winners with strong cardio and volume striking or grappling. Lock in your highest-confidence plays and let the optimizer fill around them with correlated builds.

For tournaments, embrace variance. Widen your projection ranges, increase the number of lineups generated, and don't be afraid of low-owned fighters with finishing upside. The key is balancing leverage against sound fight analysis—you want contrarian exposure that's backed by legitimate paths to victory, not random dart throws.

Consider correlation in your builds. If you're playing a fighter who typically wins by submission, stacking them with other grapplers can make sense if the slate features multiple grappling-heavy matchups. Conversely, if you project a card with multiple early finishes, loading up on favorites with knockout power creates a differentiated lineup structure.
