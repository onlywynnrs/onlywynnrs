Player Props Worth Targeting

Soccer props have exploded in popularity throughout 2026, and for good reason—they offer sharp bettors ways to exploit specific matchup advantages without needing to predict final scores.

Shots on target props deserve close attention tonight. Look at wingers and attacking midfielders facing defensive units that allow high shot volumes from wide areas. In possession-heavy games, playmakers who drift into pockets between lines often exceed their shot prop totals, especially when facing opponents who sit deep and invite pressure.

Goalkeeper save props present another angle worth exploring. When strong defensive teams face opponents with high shot volumes but low conversion rates, keeper save totals can offer value on the over. Conversely, games featuring clinical finishers against shaky defenses might see keepers fall short of inflated save lines.

Corner props tied to specific players taking set pieces also warrant consideration. Teams with dedicated corner takers who stay on the field for full 90-minute shifts provide consistency that recreational bettors often undervalue. When these players face opponents who concede corners at above-average rates, the math starts working in your favor.

Parlay Construction Strategy for Soccer

The key to long-term parlay success isn't just finding individual value bets—it's understanding how to combine them intelligently. Soccer's lower-scoring nature compared to basketball or football means correlation matters even more.

Same-game parlays (SGPs) work best when you're combining outcomes that logically support each other. Pairing a team to win with their star striker to record a shot on target makes sense. Combining a team to win with under goals creates conflict unless you're betting a defensive team in a tactical battle.

For multi-game parlays spanning tonight's MLS slate, consider mixing match result picks with totals that reflect different game scripts. A defensive battle might pair well with a high-scoring affair in your parlay construction, giving you exposure to different types of value rather than banking entirely on one game script repeating.

Avoid the trap of adding legs just to chase bigger payouts. A focused three-leg parlay built on genuine edges beats a six-leg prayer that requires perfect conditions. The sportsbooks set those massive parlay payouts high for a reason—they know most bettors are giving back edge with every additional leg.
