Profitable MLB Prop Betting Strategies

MLB prop markets have expanded dramatically, giving sharp bettors countless edges. Pitcher strikeout props remain the most popular, but savvy players also target batter props, team totals, and first inning outcomes.

For strikeout props, focus on more than just season averages. Recent form matters immensely in baseball. A pitcher who's thrown 95+ pitches in consecutive starts may have diminished velocity. Similarly, opposing team strikeout rates against specific pitch types can reveal hidden value.

Batter props require understanding platoon splits and ballpark factors. A left-handed slugger facing a right-handed fly ball pitcher in a hitter-friendly park creates obvious edges for home run and total bases props. Today's games feature several of these exploitable matchups where implied probability doesn't match actual likelihood.

Team total props often provide better value than traditional run lines because oddsmakers focus more attention on sides. When strong offensive teams face vulnerable bullpens, team total overs become particularly attractive in the middle innings.

Building Correlated Same-Game Parlays

Same-game parlays have revolutionized baseball betting by allowing correlated outcomes in single wagers. The key is understanding which outcomes genuinely correlate rather than just appearing related.

A classic positive correlation: combining a team's run line with their star pitcher's strikeout over. When an ace dominates with high strikeouts, their team is more likely to win by multiple runs. Another strong correlation involves pairing a team's first five innings lead with the game's under total.

Negative correlations matter too. Avoid pairing a heavy favorite's run line with a high game total—blowouts typically mean fewer runs than close games. Similarly, don't combine both teams' star hitters exceeding their total bases props unless you're also taking the over on the game total.
