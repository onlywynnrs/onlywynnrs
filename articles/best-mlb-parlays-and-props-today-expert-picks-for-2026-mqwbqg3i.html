Building Smarter MLB Parlays

The most successful MLB parlays balance risk and correlation intelligently. Rather than throwing together five random moneyline favorites, construct parlays around specific narratives. If you're betting on a high-scoring game environment, combine the game over with hitter props and run-line plays that all benefit from offensive production.

The Detroit Tigers vs Houston Astros game might offer angles for either approach depending on your analysis of the pitching matchup and recent form.

Two and three-leg parlays generally offer better long-term value than massive longshots. Focus on finding two or three props or outcomes where you've identified genuine edge, then combine them for enhanced odds without drowning in variance.
