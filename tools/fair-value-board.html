What is a fair value board for prediction markets?

On Polymarket and other prediction markets, a contract price is a probability — 60¢ means the market thinks there's a 60% chance. This board computes the fair probability for each side of today's games by taking the sharpest sportsbook moneylines, removing the bookmaker's margin (the "vig" or "hold"), and normalizing both sides to sum to 100%. The result is a clean, no-vig fair price you can compare directly to a live contract.

How do I find value with it?

Find the game on Polymarket, check the live contract price, and compare it to the fair price here. If a side is trading below its fair price, the market may be underpricing it — potential value. If it's trading above, you're likely overpaying. The biggest gaps tend to show up on less-liquid markets and on popular teams that carry a brand premium.
